CIAS

China Intercontinental Air Systems

An actively growing Chinese carrier with big ambitions, flying from Shanghai Pudong and Beijing Capital with further bases across the mainland from Kunming to Ürümqi.

Profile

The airline, in brief.

China Intercontinental Air Systems connects mainland China with every inhabited continent. It flies from Shanghai Pudong and Beijing Capital, with further bases at Kunming, Ürümqi, Yantai and Shenzhen.

The airline was founded in Shanghai on 16 January 2014 and began flying on 22 June 2015 as Shanghai Express, linking Shanghai with the rest of China on a small fleet of Boeing 717s, 737s and 757s.

A reorganisation in 2017 turned it towards international flying. The older aircraft were retired in favour of new types, and Chinese-built aircraft now make up a large part of the fleet.

Network

Where it flies.

Long-haul flying is split between the two primary hubs. Beijing Capital serves Africa, the Gulf, Europe, Oceania and North America; Shanghai Pudong links China with Southeast Asia, the Middle East, Europe, Oceania and Latin America.
