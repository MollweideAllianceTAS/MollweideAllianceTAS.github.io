Home / Join the alliance

Two ways in, both from inside the game.

Membership is arranged in The Airline Simulator itself. There is nothing to fill in on this site: find the group in the game's alliance search, apply there, and join the Discord channel.

The two groups

Mollweide Alliance, and Mollweide Connect.

Each is listed separately in game. Search for the name exactly as it is written below — these strings are what the game matches on, and a near miss returns nothing.

How it works

Four steps, in this order.

The whole process runs through the game and the Discord channel. Nothing is submitted here.

  1. 01

    Find the group in game

    Open the alliance search and type the name for whichever group you are applying to. Both are given above, and both can be copied straight from this page.

  2. 02

    Apply from inside the game

    Send your application through the game. Applications are reviewed by the alliance rather than accepted automatically, so expect a short wait.

  3. 03

    Join the Discord channel

    The invite links are provided in game, not on this site. Joining the channel is a condition of membership — it is where the alliance is coordinated and where everything published on this site is sent.

  4. 04

    Send your airline's details

    Once you are in, your carrier is added to this site: the member wall, the route map, and flight search as an operating carrier. The submissions page lists the six things required and the specification the livery artwork has to meet.

Discord

The channel is not optional.

Every member airline is required to be in it, and to stay in it for as long as they are a member.

  • The invite is given in game. It is not published here, and it is not shared outside the alliance.
  • Membership of the channel is a condition of membership of the alliance, for both groups.
  • Everything that appears on this site is sent there — airline details, livery artwork, lounges and press releases.

An airline that leaves the channel is treated as having left the alliance, because there is no other way to reach it.

If you have applied in game and cannot find the invite, say so in your application. Someone will send it to you directly rather than leave you waiting.
