EuroStar

A full-service carrier headquartered in Brussels, flying from Bridgetown, Warsaw, Athens and London Heathrow since 1987.

The airline, in brief.

EuroStar is a full-service international airline headquartered in Brussels, flying regional, short-haul and long-haul services across Europe, North America, the Caribbean and the Middle East from hubs at Bridgetown, Warsaw, Athens and London Heathrow.

Founded in 1987, it operated independently for decades until the 2020 downturn grounded much of its fleet and brought it close to bankruptcy. TAA Group then acquired the airline, providing the support it needed to survive and rebuild its network.

Under TAA Group ownership it still operates as its own airline, with a distinct brand, fleet, staff and passenger experience.

Where it flies.

Heathrow carries its strongest long-haul markets, Dubai and New York JFK. Bridgetown to Warsaw, one of its most distinctive routes, links the Caribbean directly with Central Europe.
