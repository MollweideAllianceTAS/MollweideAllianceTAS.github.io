Caucausair

A genuinely tri-national carrier, operating coordinated hubs in Tbilisi, Baku and Yerevan, with Batumi as a summer focus city — the alliance's bridge between Europe and Central Asia.

Profile

The airline, in brief.

Caucausair connects the South Caucasus with Europe, Asia, the Middle East and North America. Headquartered in Tbilisi, it flies from three hubs at Tbilisi, Baku and Yerevan.

The airline was formed in 2021 by the merger of Georgia's Orbeliani Airways, which flew from Tbilisi and Batumi, and Azerbaijan's Şirvan Hava Yolları of Baku. The later acquisition of Air Ararat brought Yerevan in as a third hub and made Caucausair a carrier for the whole region.

Since then it has replaced the aircraft it inherited with newer, more efficient Airbus, Embraer and ATR types.
