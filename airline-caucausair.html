Caucausair

A genuinely tri-national carrier, operating coordinated hubs in Tbilisi, Baku, Yerevan and Batumi — the alliance's bridge between Europe and Central Asia.

The airline, in brief.

Caucausair connects the South Caucasus with Europe, Asia, the Middle East and North America. Headquartered in Tbilisi, it flies from four hubs at Tbilisi, Baku, Yerevan and Batumi.

The airline was formed in 2021 by the merger of Georgia's Orbeliani Airways, which flew from Tbilisi and Batumi, and Azerbaijan's Şirvan Hava Yolları of Baku. The later acquisition of Air Ararat brought Yerevan in as a third hub and made Caucausair a carrier for the whole region.

Since then it has replaced the aircraft it inherited with newer, more efficient Airbus, Embraer and ATR types.

Where it flies.

Tbilisi carries the long-haul flying to Europe, East and Southeast Asia and New York. Baku reaches India, western Europe, Istanbul and three points in the United States; Yerevan serves Los Angeles, São Paulo, Nairobi, Istanbul, Paris, Marseille and Lyon; and Batumi links Georgia's coast with Moscow and Istanbul.
