Pacific Sunset

Pacific Sunset Airlines

The founder of the Pacific Sunset Group, grown from a Long Beach regional carrier into a worldwide airline with hubs on every inhabited continent.

The airline, in brief.

Pacific Sunset is a United States carrier and the founding airline of the Pacific Sunset Group, which includes six other airlines. Despite its name it flies worldwide, with hubs on every inhabited continent.

The airline began as a regional carrier at Long Beach, California, then expanded to Los Angeles, Honolulu, Maui, Atlanta and Seattle on its way to becoming a major international airline.

Together with its subsidiary Sunset Express, it serves every airport in California, with a fleet that runs from the Airbus A380 down to the Dash 8 Q400.

Where it flies.

Its hubs are listed by passengers carried. Los Angeles is the largest, followed by London Heathrow, São Paulo and Sydney; Long Beach, where the airline began, is still among the busiest.

Further hubs at Tokyo, San Francisco, Honolulu, Anchorage, Cape Town, San Juan, Seattle, Maui, Gran Canaria and Perth spread the network across the Pacific, the Atlantic and the Indian Ocean.
