Romanian Airways

Romania's full-service international carrier, flying from Bucharest and four regional hubs to Europe, North America and beyond.

The airline, in brief.

Romanian Airways is a full-service international airline, founded in 1954 and headquartered in Bucharest, owned by Romanian Airways Group.

It connects Romania with Europe, North America and beyond, pairing Romanian hospitality with a modern fleet. "Romania to the world."

Where it flies.

Bucharest is the main hub, with Craiova, Constanța, Timișoara and Brașov alongside it. Its 47 aircraft fly domestic, European and long-haul services, led by London Heathrow, Paris and New York JFK.
