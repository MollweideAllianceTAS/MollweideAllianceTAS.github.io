Avni Airlines

India's international flag carrier in the alliance, flying a multi-hub network led by New Delhi and Bengaluru, with overseas bases from Colombo and Bali to Georgetown, Paramaribo and Muscat.

The airline, in brief.

Avni Airlines is an Indian international flag carrier, headquartered in Bengaluru, flying a multi-hub global network led by New Delhi and Bengaluru.

It was founded in Bengaluru on 11 May 2005 as Deva Air, backed by non-resident Indians who wanted lower fares and direct connections, and flew its first service, New Delhi to Bengaluru, that June. In 2009 shareholders voted to rename it Avni, the Sanskrit word for Mother Earth, for strength, stability and reliability. Avni Travels has owned and run the airline since 2010.

New Delhi became its international transfer hub, built to compete with the great hubs of the Middle East and Southeast Asia, while a dense domestic network grew around bases in Mumbai, Chennai, Hyderabad and Bengaluru. From 2015 it opened bases abroad, in Colombo, Mauritius, Bali, Georgetown, Paramaribo and Muscat.

Where it flies.

Daily long- and short-haul flights link India, Sri Lanka, Mauritius, Bali, Guyana, Suriname and Oman with the Middle East, Europe, South America, Southeast Asia and Oceania, with a focus on diaspora and tourism routes. The fleet stands at 2,481 aircraft as of 2026.
