PolAir

The alliance's Polish member, linking Central Europe to the wider network through Warsaw Chopin, with bases at Krakow and Olsztyn-Mazury serving the south and the north-east.

Profile

The airline, in brief.

PolAir is a Polish carrier flying short-, medium- and long-haul services from Warsaw Chopin, with further bases at Krakow and Olsztyn-Mazury. It connects Poland with Europe, North America, Asia, the Middle East and leisure destinations further afield.

Founded in Warsaw in 2020, the airline has grown quickly into a major European carrier with a global network.

Its aim from the outset has been to pair efficient operations with a high standard of passenger experience, and it takes pride in its Polish heritage.

Network

Where it flies.

Warsaw carries most of the long-haul flying, to New York, Chicago, Boston, Fort Lauderdale, Montréal, Tokyo, Bangkok, Delhi, Dubai and Zanzibar, alongside a European network from Reykjavík to Baku.

Krakow adds Los Angeles, Washington and Zanzibar to its European routes, and Olsztyn-Mazury links north-east Poland with Germany and Scandinavia.
