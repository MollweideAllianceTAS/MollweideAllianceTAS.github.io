PolAir

The alliance's Polish member, linking Central Europe to the wider network through Warsaw Chopin, with bases at Krakow and Olsztyn-Mazury serving the south and the north-east.

Profile

The airline, in brief.

PolAir is a Polish carrier flying short-, medium- and long-haul services from Warsaw Chopin, with further bases at Krakow and Olsztyn-Mazury. It connects Poland with Europe, Asia, North America, the Middle East and leisure destinations further afield.

The airline began as a regional project and has grown quickly into a major European carrier with a global network.

Its aim from the outset has been to pair efficient operations with a high standard of passenger experience, and it takes pride in its Polish heritage.
