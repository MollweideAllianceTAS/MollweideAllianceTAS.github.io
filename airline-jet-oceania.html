Jet Oceania

The alliance's second Australian member, operating from Brisbane with further bases at Sydney, Cairns and Perth, linking the east coast to the west and north.

Profile

The airline, in brief.

Jet Oceania is an Australian carrier flying from four hubs at Brisbane, Sydney, Perth and Cairns. It serves Oceania together with East and Southeast Asia, and offers a high standard of comfort on every flight.

The airline was founded in Brisbane on 12 September 1998.

It sets out to lead air travel across Oceania, and it stays within that focus: South Asia and West Asia are deliberately outside its network.

Network

Where it flies.

High-frequency services link the four hubs with one another and with destinations across Australia, New Zealand, the Pacific Islands and East Asia.

The principal long-haul service flies non-stop to Tokyo, without an intermediate stop such as Manila, and the same aircraft serves the airline's other long sectors to East Asia.
