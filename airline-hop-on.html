Hop on!

Hop on Airline

Montigo Air's low-cost sister, flying short, simple hops across Italy and nearby Europe from Milan Linate.

Profile

The airline, in brief.

Hop on! is an Italian low-cost airline flying from Milan Linate, close to the city centre, to destinations across Italy and nearby Europe. It is the sister airline of Montigo Air within the Montigo Aviation Group.

Founded on 1 April 2025, it keeps short flights simple and honest: a small personal item travels free, cabin bags can be booked online in advance, and there are no costs hidden in the fine print.

Its Embraer E195-E2s are laid out two by two, so no seat is a middle seat.

Network

Where it flies.

Linate links Milan with Italy's main cities, from Venice and Trieste to Naples, Bari and Sicily, and with nearby European cities from Barcelona and Madrid to Vienna, Zagreb and Malta.

Passengers continuing further afield can connect with Montigo Air through Milan Malpensa.
