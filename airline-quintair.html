QuintAir

A US international carrier in character liveries, flying from Los Angeles, Miami, New York JFK and Dallas/Fort Worth.

Profile

The airline, in brief.

QuintAir is a United States international carrier flying from hubs at Los Angeles, Miami, New York JFK and Dallas/Fort Worth. It joined the alliance through Mollweide Connect.

Its aircraft wear character liveries inspired by the anime The Quintessential Quintuplets, giving the airline a style passengers recognise wherever it flies.

A dedicated domestic fleet is planned, to expand its regional flying.

Network

Where it flies.

QuintAir flies long-haul from all four hubs: across the Pacific to Tokyo, Seoul and Sydney, across the Atlantic to London, and south to São Paulo. Its fleet is led by the Boeing 777-300ER, 787-9 and Airbus A350-1000.
