Union Airlines

A mainline US carrier linking the American South with the rest of the country and abroad, from Nashville and Boston.

The airline, in brief.

Union Airlines is a mainline United States carrier connecting the southern United States with the rest of the country and abroad, from hubs at Nashville and Boston.

It puts comfort, safety and reliability first.

Where it flies.

Nashville is the larger hub, with long-haul flights to London Heathrow, Paris, Frankfurt, Madrid and Honolulu alongside a domestic network reaching Los Angeles, Denver, Miami, Atlanta and across the South. Boston links New England with Chicago, Minneapolis, Seattle, Salt Lake City and the Midwest.
