01 · The meaning

A map projection became
a way of seeing the world.

Named after an equal-area map projection, Mollweide brings airlines from different regions together to share opportunities and make their networks more visible.

01 Entering the network 02 Connecting airlines 03 Growing together

Equal-area by design

Every market has its place.

The Mollweide projection preserves the relative area of regions across the world. It gives the alliance its name and reflects the breadth of markets represented by its members.

Member visibility

Give your airline a wider audience.

Member profiles introduce airlines, their hubs and routes to the wider alliance and to visitors exploring the network.

Working together

More opportunities to connect.

Members can find codeshare partners, coordinate routes and take part in a growing market, with discounts of up to 70% on offers.
